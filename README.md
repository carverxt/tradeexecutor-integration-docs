## The Problem: The Execution Gap

Most traders have a better strategy than their results show. The gap between what the strategy says and what actually happens is the execution gap — caused by:

- **Latency**: Signal fires. You see it 45 seconds later. The entry is gone.
- **Emotion**: Strategy says -2% exit. You hold to -3.1%. That 1.1% compounds.
- **Inconsistency**: You follow the system Monday. After a bad week, you deviate.
- **Missed trades**: 2am signal. You were asleep.

TradeExecutor eliminates all four.
